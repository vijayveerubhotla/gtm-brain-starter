---
name: decision-review
description: Turn observed GTM evidence into a bounded reviewed decision with comparison, maturity, limits and rollback. Use for budget, routing, scoring or content changes.
---

Read the measurement definition, relevant job contract and previous decision record. Separate observed facts from the hypothesis. Check sample size, maturity, missing outcomes and changed conditions before proposing a change.

Write the question, one intervention, primary metric, comparison, maturity, spend or workload limit, hold condition, owner and review date. At review, record counts and choose keep, reverse, extend or retire with a reason. Inconclusive is a valid result.

Update the company rule only after its owner accepts the evidence. Preserve the previous version and rollback path. Drafting a decision does not authorize publishing, sending or live-system writes.
