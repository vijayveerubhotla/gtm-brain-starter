name: signal-decay
description: Validate signal event dates and apply the company-approved freshness model while preserving source and uncertainty. Use for timing reviews and expiring stale company signals.
---

Read the approved signal model and an explicit as-of date. Use actual event date for age; preserve discovery date separately. Verify relevance and source before adding timing points.

Reject future or invalid dates, unsupported signals and confidence outside the agreed range. Apply the approved decay and expiry rules, cap the aggregate and keep the calculation readable. The starter's half-life is illustrative, not a universal buying-cycle claim.

Return event age, effective points, expiry state, source and a research or review disposition. Contradictory observations go to a person. A fresh signal does not repair poor fit or override suppression or ownership.
