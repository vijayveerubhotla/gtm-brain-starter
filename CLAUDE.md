# Company GTM operating system

Read this entry point before a GTM job. Confirm the company and the job's output. Use one company brain; lane plugins share the approved context.

Company context: `context/company.md`, `context/icp.md`, `context/personas.md`, `context/positioning.md` and `context/measurement.md`.

Bounded jobs: skills in `.claude/skills/`; lane contract in `plugins/example-lane/README.md`; review command in `commands/run-review.md`; deterministic example in `scripts/evaluate_example.py`.

Evidence rules: `references/evidence-rules.md`. Connector access: `connectors/README.md`. Decisions: `memory/decisions.csv`.

Facts need a source and observed date. Mark hypotheses and unknowns. A fluent answer is not proof. Exclusions, suppression, current ownership and human review are read before proposing activation. Low confidence goes to research.

Write outputs to a private working folder chosen for the task. This public starter provides no authorization for live CRM changes, email sending or publication.
