# Evidence rules

Store source and observation date for material facts. Preserve an event's actual date separately from its discovery date. A missing field stays unknown; it does not earn points because a model can infer a plausible value.

Keep company fit separate from timing. Exclusions override scores. A signal needs a relevant source and cannot prove private priorities. Contradictions go to a person rather than being averaged away.

Never treat newsletter consent, a company website match or a public reaction as automatic permission for sales outreach. Check applicable company rules and current provider requirements for the actual job.

Calibrate only against mature outcomes with pre-outcome facts. Record the sample and limits. Local fixture success proves the example's behavior, not pipeline performance.
